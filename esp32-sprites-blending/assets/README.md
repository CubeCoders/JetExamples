# Original procedural artwork

The cafe sign and two quarter-halo images are generated by
`../tools/prepare_assets.py` and supplied under the examples repository's MIT
licence. Scene geometry, moon, sculpture, plants and fake-light meshes are
procedural. No downloaded artwork or model is used.

The 128x48 sign occupies 12 KiB as RGB565. Each 16x16 halo quarter occupies
512 bytes, making 13 KiB of world-sign/halo pixels in total. Label sprites are
additional immutable flash data. A local monospace font is used to generate the
sign and labels; the font file is not distributed. Supply `--font` to choose
another installed font.
